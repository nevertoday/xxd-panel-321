## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is an isolated vignette from an old herbal, a folk manual or a private seal. Hand-inked line varies and pauses. Solid ink fills and organic shapes carry the spirit, not realistic proportion. A star, a plant or a small abstract mark may serve the subject and must not build a ground. The mark sits alone in a large blank. Reject a full illustration, smooth vector, a commercial emblem and decorative clutter.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
